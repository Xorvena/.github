## 🌟 Featured Project

### TRUSTLOOP AI

**Let AI buy. Let humans stay in control.**

An AI-powered financial governance platform for autonomous commerce.

TRUSTLOOP AI explores how AI shopping agents can make purchase recommendations and initiate transactions while remaining governed by user-defined financial policies, spending limits, risk controls, and human approval workflows.

**Core areas:**
- AI-powered shopping assistance.
- Deterministic financial policy enforcement.
- Risk assessment and agent trust management.
- Human-in-the-loop purchase authorization.
- PayPal Sandbox payment integration.
- Transparent transaction auditing.

[Explore TRUSTLOOP AI](https://github.com/XORVENA/trustloop-ai)
